# IMD Heavy Rainfall Categories, Urban Flood Safety & Waterlogging Precautions

## IMD Quantitative Precipitation Categories (24-Hour Accumulation)
- **Very Light Rain**: 0.1 mm to 2.4 mm
- **Light Rain**: 2.5 mm to 7.5 mm
- **Moderate Rain**: 7.6 mm to 35.5 mm
- **Rather Heavy Rain**: 35.6 mm to 64.4 mm
- **Heavy Rain (Yellow/Orange Alert)**: 64.5 mm to 115.5 mm
- **Very Heavy Rain (Orange/Red Alert)**: 115.6 mm to 204.4 mm
- **Extremely Heavy Rain (Red Alert)**: >= 204.5 mm

## Urban Inundation and Rural Waterlogging Directives
- **Low-lying Underpasses**: Avoid walking or driving through standing water of unknown depth; fast-flowing water of merely 15 cm can sweep away pedestrians and 30 cm can float vehicles.
- **Agricultural Land**: Open field drainage culverts before heavy downpours. Stagnant water exceeding 48 hours asphyxiates root systems of pulses, cotton, and vegetables.
- **Drinking Water Safety**: Boil or chlorinate surface and shallow well water during floods to prevent waterborne dysentery and cholera outbreaks.
