# NDMA National Heatwave Guidelines & Thermal Stress Management

## Heatwave Criteria in India
- **Plains**: Maximum temperature reaching >= 40.0°C and departure from normal >= 4.5°C (or absolute maximum >= 45.0°C).
- **Coastal Stations**: Maximum temperature reaching >= 37.0°C and departure from normal >= 4.5°C.
- **Hilly Regions**: Maximum temperature reaching >= 30.0°C.

## Thermal Comfort & Agricultural Protection
- **Personal Hydration**: Drink ample water, ORS, coconut water, lemon water, and buttermilk. Avoid direct outdoor exposure between 12:00 PM and 04:00 PM.
- **Livestock Care**: Keep dairy cattle in ventilated sheds with roof thatch or sprinklers. Provide cool water and mineral salt licks.
- **Irrigation Strategy**: Irrigate crops strictly during early dawn or late evening to reduce evaporative losses and avoid soil thermal shock to roots.
