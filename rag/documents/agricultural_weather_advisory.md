# Agricultural Meteorology & Crop Protection Principles (ICAR / IMD Agromet)

## 1. Sowing & Moisture Windows
- **Optimal Sowing Moisture**: Soil should be at field capacity (50-70% volumetric moisture) without water pooling.
- **Pre-sowing Rule**: Sowing immediately before an anticipated heavy downpour (> 30 mm) leads to seed displacement, crust formation, and damping-off disease.

## 2. Agrochemical Spraying Restrictions
- **Rain Washout**: Do not spray insecticides, fungicides, or foliar urea if rain is forecast within 4 to 6 hours.
- **Wind Drift**: Postpone spraying if surface winds exceed 15 km/h to prevent spray drift onto neighboring crops and operator inhalation.
- **High Temperature Spraying**: Avoid applying micronutrients during noon heat (> 35°C) to prevent foliar chemical scorch.

## 3. Irrigation Scheduling Principles
- **Rain Forecast Offset**: If forecast predicts >= 15 mm rain within 48 hours, suspend irrigation cycles to save groundwater and power.
- **Critical Stages**: Crown root initiation in wheat (21 days after sowing), tillering in rice, and boll formation in cotton must never suffer severe moisture stress.
