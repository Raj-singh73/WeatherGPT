# IMD & NDMA Cyclone Warning Stages & Safety Directives

## Official Cyclone Warning Stages in India
1. **Pre-Cyclone Watch (72 hours prior)**: Issued by the Director General of Meteorology (DGM) indicating the formation of a cyclonic disturbance in the Bay of Bengal or Arabian Sea and its likely development and track.
2. **Cyclone Alert (Yellow Message - 48 hours prior)**: Issued to coastal state administrations when a cyclone is expected to commence adverse weather along the coast.
3. **Cyclone Warning (Orange Message - 24 hours prior)**: Issued specifying anticipated point of landfall, wind speed, storm surge height, and anticipated damage.
4. **Post-Landfall Outlook (Red Message - 12 hours prior)**: Issued regarding interior inundation, severe gales, and flash flood impact as the storm moves inland.

## Citizen & Farmer Safety Measures
- **Shelter**: Evacuate low-lying coastal flood zones and shift to cyclone relief shelters immediately upon orange/red warnings.
- **Agriculture**: Harvest mature standing crops immediately. Anchor banana, papaya, and betel vine gardens. Secure agricultural machinery in concrete garages.
- **Power & Communication**: Disconnect domestic power mains to prevent electrocution during gale gusts. Keep mobile devices fully charged and preserve emergency radio batteries.
- **Maritime**: Strictly enforce complete suspension of fishing and small watercraft operations in deep sea and coastal shelves.
