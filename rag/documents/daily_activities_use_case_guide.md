# Daily Activities & Practical Weather Decision Standards

## Laundry & Outdoor Clothes Drying
- **Safe Conditions**: Precipitation probability < 20%, expected rain < 1.0 mm, relative humidity < 70%, and moderate wind (5-20 km/h).
- **Optimal Drying Window**: Between 10:00 AM and 3:30 PM under direct solar irradiance.
- **Unfavorable Conditions**: Relative humidity > 75%, overcast skies with dew point within 2°C of ambient temperature, or rain forecast within 6 hours. Wet clothes will develop damp mildew odor and fail to dry.

## Car & Vehicle Washing
- **Safe Conditions**: No rain forecast (> 1.0 mm) for at least the next 48 to 72 hours, wind speed < 25 km/h (low dust swirl).
- **Postponement Threshold**: If precipitation > 1.0 mm is predicted within 48 hours, washing is counterproductive as road splash and mud spray will immediately re-soil the vehicle.

## Outdoor Sports, Cricket & Recreation
- **Optimal Playing Window**: Early morning (6:30 AM – 9:30 AM) or late afternoon (4:30 PM – 6:30 PM).
- **Safety Hazards**: 
  - Wet ground / pitch slippage if rainfall in past 24h exceeds 5.0 mm.
  - Heat Index / Apparent Temperature > 38°C causes severe heat exhaustion and dehydration.
  - Wind gusts > 35 km/h or convective thunderstorm clouds (lightning hazard within 15 km requires suspension).

## Outdoor Social Events, Tents & Weddings
- **Structural Wind Limit**: Commercial shamianas and marquees should not exceed maximum wind gust threshold of 35-40 km/h without reinforced anchoring.
- **Precipitation Safeguards**: Cumulative 3-day rainfall > 15 mm creates sodden ground, lawn sinking, and electrical short-circuit hazards. Waterproof canopies and elevated wooden decking required.

## Construction, Concrete Pouring & Exterior Painting
- **Exterior Painting Curing**: Requires minimum 24-48 continuous rain-free hours with relative humidity strictly below 80% and surface temperature between 10°C and 35°C. High humidity causes blistering, streaking, and adhesion failure.
- **Concrete Slab Pouring**: Heavy rain within 6-12 hours of pouring washes away cement paste (slurry wash), weakening structural tensile strength.

## Highway Transit, Fog & Low Visibility Driving
- **Radiation Fog Conditions**: Dew point depression (Temperature minus Dew Point) < 2.0°C with clear night skies and wind < 8 km/h. Highly prevalent across Indo-Gangetic Plains (Dec–Feb).
- **Safe Commute Directives**: Use low-beam headlights and yellow fog lamps. Maintain 4x standard braking distance. Avoid sudden lane changes on expressways when visibility falls below 200 meters.

## Agriculture: Crop Harvesting & Threshing
- **Harvest Window Criteria**: Minimum 3 consecutive rain-free days with relative humidity < 60% during daylight hours.
- **Grain Quality Preservation**: Harvested grains must reach safe storage moisture (< 12-14% for wheat/paddy). Wet harvesting promotes Aspergillus fungus and aflatoxin contamination.
